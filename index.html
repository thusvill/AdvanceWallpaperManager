ESSENTIAL FEATURES

The parts that matter.

Real depth layering

Base wallpaper, custom clock, then the detected subject on top.

ML Kit subject cutout

On-device ML Kit segmentation creates the foreground mask for the depth effect.

Your font, your clock, your wallpapers

Use system fonts or import TTF, OTF, and ZIP font archives.

One portable config

Bundle the image, mask, preview, font, and settings into one shareable file.

Wallpaper rotation

Daily, hourly, or on-awake rotation powered by Android WorkManager.

Built to stay quiet

The lockscreen clock refreshes by minute boundary instead of running a constant UI loop.

IMPORTANT

Segmentation can be imperfect.

ML Kit does not reproduce Apple's depth extraction. Hair, thin edges, similar-colored objects, and overlapping subjects can be misread. Some photos will need mask tuning, and some simply will not produce a clean depth effect.
